# Privacy notice · ITC Club Hub
Version 0.1 · 2026-11-09 · Author: Suonghan Sethaleaph

## Who we are
We are a team of ITC students who built Club Hub to help clubs run events.

## What we collect and why
- **Student ID, name, email:** to create your account and register you for events.
- **Club memberships:** so you only see events of your clubs.
- **Attendance:** so clubs can plan rooms and events.
- **Phone number (optional):** only if you agree to SMS reminders.
- **Your choices:** whether you said yes to email or SMS reminders.

We collect nothing else.

## Who sees it
- Organisers see your name and a masked student ID.
- Club leaders see attendance for their own events only.
- Administrators see what they need to run Club Hub.
- Our SMS service sees your phone number, only to send your reminders.

We do not sell your data. Logs never show your name, email or phone.

## How long
- We keep your account data until 6 months after you graduate.
- After one academic year, attendance becomes anonymous counts.
- We erase your phone number as soon as you withdraw SMS consent.

## Your choices
- Email and SMS reminders are off unless you turn them on.
- Saying "no" never stops you from joining.
- You can withdraw consent any time, in My profile or the CLI.
- You can ask to see or delete your data.
