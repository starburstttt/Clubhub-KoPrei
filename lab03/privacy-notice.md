# Privacy notice · ITC Club Hub
Version 0.1 · 2026-11-09 · Author: Suonghan Sethaleaph

## Who we are
We are the ITC Club Hub team, a student club at our university.

## What we collect and why
- **Student ID, name, email:** to create your account and contact you about club activities.
- **Phone number (optional):** only if you agree to SMS reminders.
- **Your choices:** we store whether you said yes to email or SMS reminders.

We collect nothing else.

## Who sees it
Only the club officers who run the Hub. We do not sell or share your data. Logs never show your name, email or phone.

## How long
- We keep your account data while you are a member.
- We delete it within 30 days after you leave.
- We erase your phone number as soon as you withdraw SMS consent.

## Your choices
- Email and SMS reminders are off unless you turn them on.
- Saying "no" never stops you from joining.
- You can withdraw consent at any time, in the CLI or on the web form.
- You can ask us to see or delete your data.

Email us at clubhub-privacy@example.edu.
